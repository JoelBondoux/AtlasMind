## Describe the project in your own words

Everything else AtlasMind knows about a project it works out from the code. What the project is **for** it cannot — that exists only in your head. So it asks, once:

- **Who is it for?**
- **What should it do?**
- **What should be true once it works?**

A couple of sentences is enough. The answer is saved to `project_memory/project-brief.md` **exactly as you wrote it** — never summarised, tidied or rewritten. Skipping is fine: it is recorded as not given, and nothing is filled in for you.

### From the brief to the roadmap

Open **Project Ideation** and the *From your brief* card shows the chain, what each step would write, and which one is next:

1. **Read the brief into cards.** A card either quotes your brief word for word or is a question. You see every card before any is added.
2. **Raise grounded cards as roadmap items.** Only cards that quote your brief can become work, and you pick which. Questions stay on the board.
3. **File a plan for each item** *(optional)*. Empty frames of questions, created only where none exists.
4. **Quote the brief in the project soul** *(optional)*. Appended once, never edited; nothing already in the file changes.

Each step is its own confirmation, and the chain picks up where your files say it is.
