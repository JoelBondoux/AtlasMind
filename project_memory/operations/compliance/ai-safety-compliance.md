## Controls

Every row starts at **Not assessed**, which is deliberately not the same as *compliant*.
An unassessed control and a satisfied one are different facts, and seeding a pass would
assert something nobody checked. Set a status only when you have looked.

Status is one of: `Not assessed` · `Satisfied` · `Partial` · `Gap` · `Not applicable`.
A `Not applicable` needs a justification in the Evidence column — that is what an
assessor will ask for.

| Ref | Requirement | Status | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GOV-1` | AI system role classified (provider / deployer) and risk tier stated | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-2` | Model or system card published and current | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-3` | Human oversight mechanism defined for consequential outputs | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-4` | Declared guardrails have a corresponding enforcement test | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-5` | Red-team evidence retained with dates and scope | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-6` | Incident reporting route defined for AI-specific failures | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
| `GOV-7` | Training-data provenance and permitted use recorded | Not assessed | _none recorded_ | _unassigned_ |
