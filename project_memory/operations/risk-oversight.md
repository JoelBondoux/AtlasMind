# Risk Oversight

> Maintained by AtlasMind (Project Dashboard → Risk). This is the human-readable
> mirror of `risk-oversight.json`; both are regenerated together from the dashboard.

These findings are produced by AtlasMind's ethics, legal, and commercial oversight
advisors. **They are advisory only and are not professional advice** — they are
structured prompts for human judgement, not a clearance to proceed. Anything
consequential needs qualified review: counsel in the relevant jurisdiction for legal
exposure, an ethics or DPO review for human impact, and finance or commercial
sign-off for business commitments.

Nothing here blocks a commit, a promotion, or a release. Findings are never deleted —
they change status — so this file stays a complete record of what was raised and what
was decided.
