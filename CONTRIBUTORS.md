# Contributors

AtlasMind remains open source for everyone. This file is for optional public acknowledgement only.

The sections below match the [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/JoelBondoux) tiers, so a
listing says which ask somebody answered and nothing more. Nobody appears here without saying so.

## Supporters

Individuals, students, and anyone supporting AtlasMind outside commercial work — the voluntary
$5/month tier, and one-off contributions of any size.

No public supporters listed yet.

## Maintenance Fee organizations

Organizations paying the [Open Source Maintenance Fee](MAINTENANCE_FEE.md), which comes into force at
**v1.0.0** under the terms in [OSMFEULA.txt](OSMFEULA.txt). Listed without their tier, because the
tier is a headcount band and publishing it would say something about the organization rather than
about their support.

No organizations listed yet — no fee is payable during Beta.

## Acknowledgement Policy

- **Public listing is opt-in**, and off by default. Say the word and you are listed; say nothing and
  nothing is published. You can ask to be removed at any time.
- **One-off contributors can be listed** under Supporters if they want to be.
- **Listings do not carry a date or a duration.** Wording like "supported from the very beginning"
  stops being true for everyone who arrives later, and quietly ranks people by when they found the
  project rather than by what they did.
- **Funding grants no exclusive access, no priority, and no vote**, and does not alter the MIT
  licence. Nothing on this page is a tier of the product, because there is no tier of the product.
